# Balance methods

Read when explaining a balance flag.

- **Cost curve**: count cards per cost. Gaps leave turns with nothing to play; one cost holding a third or more of the set narrows choices. Tools like Cubealyzer chart the same thing for Magic cubes.
- **Vanilla test** (Magic community heuristic): judge a creature's stats against its cost alone, about two stat points per mana plus a small base, then treat abilities as upside. It finds outliers to read, not verdicts.
- **Rarity bands**: Slay the Spire card rewards are about 60% common, 37% uncommon, 3% rare after normal fights, 50/40/10 after elites, all rare from bosses, with a pity counter that starts at -5% and adds 1% per non-rare reward. A set's rarity mix and its reward odds are separate dials; check both.
- **Simulate** when numbers disagree with feel: ludowright runs Monte Carlo balance on a game model.
